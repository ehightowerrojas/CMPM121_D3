# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state, use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case add a step here.

| #  | Action                                                                         | Expected observation                                                                                                                                     |
| -- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Load the page                                                                  | One canvas. A pale marker at the center of a green map. No console errors.                                                                               |
| 2  | Tap the right arrow                                                            | The marker slides one cell east. `#live` says `moved to 1, 0`. `player` is `{ x: 1, y: 0 }`.                                                             |
| 3  | Walk to the edge of the starting island and press toward the shallow water     | The marker bumps back. `#live` says `blocked by tideflat ... (need Reed Skiff)`. The player stays on the starting island.                                |
| 4  | Tap an arrow during a walk                                                     | The tap waits. The marker finishes the walk, then takes that step. It never skips a cell.                                                                |
| 5  | Mash arrows quickly, then stop                                                 | The marker settles on the cell `player` reports.                                                                                                         |
| 6  | Hold an arrow down                                                             | The marker keeps walking. Holding into a wall repeats the bump.                                                                                          |
| 7  | Load `?seed=7`                                                                 | A different map.                                                                                                                                         |
| 8  | Run `game.state` in the console                                                | Plain data: `seed`, `player`, `resources`, `gatheredCells`, `items`, and `ended`. No terrain list.                                                       |
| 9  | Press an arrow several times                                                   | Position and Terrain in the panel change as each step plays, and never ahead of the marker.                                                              |
| 10 | Make the window short, then press the down arrow                               | The page does not scroll. The marker moves.                                                                                                              |
| 11 | Click the sound button, then press an arrow                                    | The button reads `Sound: off`, and the arrow still moves the marker.                                                                                     |
| 12 | Click the sound button again                                                   | The button reads `Sound: on`, and you hear a two-note chime.                                                                                             |
| 13 | Tab to the sound button and press Space                                        | Space on the sound button toggles it rather than gathering. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                            |
| 14 | Listen while walking and bumping (headphones or speakers)                      | A soft tick on each step, and a low buzz on each bump. **This is the only check that a person can hear them.**                                           |
| 15 | On a high-resolution display, or at browser zoom 200%                          | Grid edges stay sharp. The marker stays centered. Run `document.querySelector("canvas").width`: it is the canvas `clientWidth` times `devicePixelRatio`. |
| 16 | Stand on a gatherable cell and press Space                                     | The marker pops briefly, a short gather sound plays, the HUD count increments, `#live` reports what was gathered, and the cell fades as used.            |
| 17 | Press Space again on the same gathered cell                                    | No duplicate resource is added; `#live` reports nothing to gather.                                                                                       |
| 18 | Gather enough materials for the Skiff or Axe, then press `1` or `2`            | Spent resources are deducted in the HUD, `game.state.items` increments, a chime plays, and `#live` announces the crafted item.                           |
| 19 | Press `1`, `2`, or `3` without enough materials                                | A bump sound plays, state and HUD counts do not change, and `#live` reports what materials are missing.                                                  |
| 20 | Craft `2` (Stone Axe) once, then press `2` again                               | The second press fails with already owned, plays a bump, and leaves state and HUD counts unchanged.                                                      |
| 21 | Find the Recipes list and inspect its buttons                                  | Reed Skiff, Stone Axe, and Sturdy Raft show their ingredients and readiness; unavailable recipes remain focusable.                                       |
| 22 | Tab to a recipe button and press Enter with enough ingredients                 | The recipe crafts through the same event flow as its number key, updates the inventory, and leaves focus on the recipe button.                           |
| 23 | Click a recipe button with missing ingredients                                 | The attempt reports the missing materials, plays the failure feedback, leaves resources unchanged, and returns keyboard focus to the page.               |
| 24 | Gather reeds and driftwood on the starting island                              | The Reed Skiff recipe becomes ready; Stone Axe remains unavailable because there is no stone on this island.                                             |
| 25 | Craft the Reed Skiff, then walk across the shallow water                       | Tideflat cells become passable and the marker reaches the neighboring rocky island.                                                                      |
| 26 | Gather stone on the neighboring island and craft the Stone Axe                 | Cairnstone yields stone there, so the Axe becomes craftable only after reaching another island.                                                          |
| 27 | Before crafting the Axe, press Space on an outer island's hardwood grove       | The HUD says the Stone Axe is needed; no resource is gained and the grove cell is not consumed.                                                          |
| 28 | Craft the Axe and gather hardwood from the grove                               | Each fresh grove cell gives one hardwood; the Axe stays in the inventory.                                                                                |
| 29 | Gather the Sturdy Raft recipe's materials and craft it using its button or `3` | A victory message and sound play, the resource counts update, and `game.state.ended` becomes true.                                                       |
| 30 | After victory, try moving, gathering, and crafting                             | The marker and inventory remain unchanged and no additional events play. Refreshing starts a new game.                                                   |
| 31 | Reload, tap an arrow and Space, then inspect `game.controller.recorded`        | The list contains the commands actually sent to the core in order. Reloading clears it.                                                                  |
